Frequently asked

What currency should I bring?

Tulum runs on Mexican pesos. Carry some cash — not every restaurant takes card, and ATMs can be hard to find.

Will I get by in English?

Spanish is the official language, but you'll have no trouble getting by in English throughout Tulum town and Playa.

How do I get around?

Foot and bike cover most of Tulum beach and town. Uber doesn't operate here, but taxis are everywhere — agreeing on a price upfront is normal. For longer trips, renting a car or taking the ADO bus works well.

When's the best time to visit?

Tulum is warm and tropical year-round (75–90°F by day). There's no true bad season, though June–November brings the rainy/hurricane window.
